Common questions

Referral Program FAQ

Who can refer businesses to the Chamber?

Any current Chamber member at any tier — from Friend to Platinum. We track referrals by your member account, so make sure you're logged in or use the email tied to your membership when you submit. Staff members at member businesses can refer using the company account.

When do I get the credit?

Credit posts to your account 30 days after the referred business completes their initial membership payment and onboarding call. The 30 days protects against early cancellations and clears any chargebacks before we issue credit. You see status in real-time in your member dashboard.

Can I refer my own clients or vendors?

Yes — those are typically the highest-converting referrals. Your CPA, your printer, your insurance broker, the salon next door — anyone who genuinely benefits from the Chamber. We do not pay for self-referrals or related entities you control (e.g., a second LLC you own).

Is there a cap on referrals?

No cap on the $100-per-referral credit. The free-year bonus triggers at 5 successful referrals in any rolling 12-month period and stacks — refer 10 in a year, get two free years on us. We've had members earn 3-year free runs this way.

What counts as a "successful" referral?

A referred business who (a) was not previously a Chamber member, (b) joins at any tier within 90 days of your submission, and (c) completes their first month of membership. We email you the moment each box is checked, and the dashboard shows live status.

Can I take cash instead of a statement credit?

Statement credit is the default and is most tax-friendly. If your dues are paid up for the year, we can issue a check or a same-tier upgrade credit; just reply to your reward confirmation email and we'll route it. Most members roll the credit into a tier upgrade.
