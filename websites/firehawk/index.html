The part nobody tells you

Hardening your home can lower what you pay to insure it.

California's Safer from Wildfires regulation requires insurers who price wildfire risk to discount documented hardening — and the FAIR Plan now offers hardening discounts too. Firehawk does the work and the paperwork.

Door one

Your premiums are painful

Documented ember defense, defensible space, and roof upgrades can qualify you for recurring discounts on the wildfire portion of your premium — every year the work stands.

Door two

You've been dropped

"Fire-risk" is not a life sentence. Bringing the home to fire-compliant standards — with inspection paperwork to prove it — is how non-renewed homeowners become policyholders again.

Safer from Wildfires is a California Department of Insurance framework (Insurance Code §2644.9). Discounts apply to the wildfire portion of a premium and vary by insurer and measures completed.
