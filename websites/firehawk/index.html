The part nobody tells you

Hardening your home can lower
what you pay to insure it.

California's Safer from Wildfires regulation requires insurers who price wildfire risk to give discounts for documented hardening — and the FAIR Plan now offers hardening discounts too. Firehawk does the work and the documentation.

Door one

Your premiums are painful

Documented ember defense, defensible space, and roof upgrades can qualify you for wildfire-mitigation discounts on the wildfire portion of your premium — savings that recur every single year the work stands.

Door two

You've been dropped — or can't get covered

"Fire-risk" is not a life sentence. Bringing the home to fire-compliant standards is how non-renewed homeowners become insurable policyholders again — with the inspection paperwork to prove it.

Safer from Wildfires is a California Department of Insurance framework (Insurance Code §2644.9, effective October 2022). Discounts apply to the wildfire portion of a premium and vary by insurer and measures completed; Firehawk provides the documentation your insurer or the FAIR Plan asks for.

Real questions, straight answers

What homeowners ask us every week

How do I protect my home from embers during a wildfire? +
Close the entry points before fire season: ember-resistant mesh on gutters and vents, sealed or boxed-in eaves, and a roof rated for fire. Embers travel a mile or more ahead of a fire front — homes ignite from the top and the vents first, which is exactly where Ember Defense goes to work.
What is Zone 0, and do I have to comply yet? +
Zone 0 is the ember-resistant zone within five feet of your walls, decks, and attachments. California's statewide rule is still being finalized, but insurers already look at those five feet today — and getting them right now is cheaper than retrofitting under a deadline later.
Will home hardening actually lower my insurance bill? +
It can. Under California's Safer from Wildfires regulation, admitted insurers that price wildfire risk must offer discounts for documented mitigation, and the FAIR Plan offers hardening discounts as well. The key word is documented — we photograph, certify, and file the work so the discount actually lands.
What should I do when a Red Flag Warning is issued? +
If your home is already hardened, a Red Flag day is a checklist, not a crisis: clear anything combustible out of the first five feet, charge the batteries, confirm the cameras, and review your evacuation plan. If it isn't hardened yet — that's the assessment to book before the next warning, not during it.
My insurer dropped me. Can this house be covered again? +
In most cases, yes. Non-renewal is usually about the property's risk profile, and the risk profile is exactly what hardening changes. We bring the home to fire-compliant standards and produce the documentation that turns a "fire-risk" file into an insurable one.
