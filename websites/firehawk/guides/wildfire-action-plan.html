If you leave

Leave early — the road is part of the fire. Close all windows and doors (unlocked exterior doors help crews), leave lights on so the house is visible in smoke, and shut off gas at the meter if instructed. A Firehawk-equipped home keeps fighting after you’re gone: saturation zones on the roof, cameras you can watch from the evacuation center, batteries running it all.
