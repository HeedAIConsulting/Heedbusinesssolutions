Zone 0 — the ember-resistant zone (0–5 feet)

The five feet touching your walls decide most outcomes. California’s statewide Zone 0 rules are in draft now — homes that get compliant early do it on their own schedule and budget.

Zone 1 — lean, clean & green (5–30 feet)

Zone 2 — reduced fuel (30–100 feet)

Where the Fire Wall comes in

Clearing fuel is half the job. Firehawk’s Fire Wall treatment adds the other half: an organic fire retardant applied to the perimeter and remaining vegetation, so what stays green stays inert. Combined with a written defensible-space plan, it’s the difference between “we cut some brush” and a property a fire can’t feed on.
