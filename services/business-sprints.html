How It Works

A Sprint Built Around Your Business

Every sprint follows the same four-phase model — adapted to your specific business, team, and growth objectives — so there is always a clear structure and a clear deliverable at every stage.

01

Intake & Assessment

The sprint opens with a structured intake process — stakeholder interviews, financial review, operational assessment, market analysis, and competitive landscape scan. We learn your business deeply before recommending anything.

02

Sprint Planning

Assessment findings are synthesized into a structured sprint plan — objectives, KPIs, milestones, owner assignments, and a week-by-week schedule. You review and approve the plan before execution begins so everyone is aligned on what success looks like.

03

Execution

The sprint runs on schedule with weekly check-in calls, progress tracking against KPIs, and real-time adjustments as needed. We work alongside your team — not in a vacuum — so the execution builds internal capability at the same time it delivers results.

04

Review & Handoff

The sprint closes with a comprehensive review session — results against targets, lessons learned, open items, and a fully documented 90-day forward plan so your team can continue building momentum with confidence after the engagement ends.
