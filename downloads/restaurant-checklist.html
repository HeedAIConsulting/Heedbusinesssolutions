Date Night in the West Valley

A printable cheat sheet for the next time you need to choose well

13 chamber-member restaurants, organized by vibe. Pick the row that matches your night, call ahead, show your West Valley Loyalty Card if you have one, and enjoy.

For an actual celebration (anniversary, big news)

  • Fogo de Chão · Topanga & Califa, Woodland Hills · (818) 887-3007 · 6 weeks out for December
  • Sushi Katsu-Ya · 11680 Ventura, Studio City · (818) 985-6976 · omakase only after 7pm
  • Republique · 624 S La Brea (worth the drive) · (310) 362-6115 · French bistro · weekend brunch

For "first date that doesn't feel like a first date"

  • Casa Vega · 13301 Ventura, Sherman Oaks · margaritas, patio, since 1956
  • Trattoria Farfalla · 1978 Hillhurst, Los Feliz · romantic, homemade pasta
  • Lemonade · Canoga Ave, Woodland Hills · casual, healthy, conversation-friendly

For "this turned into dinner unexpectedly"

  • Mel's Drive-In · Ventura Blvd · open 24 hours · the safety net
  • Burgers Never Say Die · 11800 Riverside, Valley Village · craft burgers + milkshakes
  • The Habit Burger Grill · multiple Valley locations · fast + reliable

Before you go — check the box

  • Reservation made (most of the above need 24-48 hours)
  • Loyalty Card in your wallet (10% off lunch at Fogo with the card)
  • Parking plan (Westfield Topanga lot if dining in Warner Center)
  • Driver agreed (margaritas at Casa Vega = no driving home)
  • Gift / card / flowers acquired if it's the kind of night that needs them
