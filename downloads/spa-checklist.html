Self-Care Routine Planner

A printable schedule for the kind of self-care that actually happens

Three tiers — Always, Sometimes, Rarely-but-needed. Chamber-member spas and wellness providers fit each tier. Loyalty Card discounts apply at most.

Always (weekly or monthly habits)

  • 10-min daily walk — Tarzana Rec or Reseda Park if you need a destination
  • Massage every 4-6 weeks — Healing Hands WH · Massage Envy Topanga
  • Hair every 4-8 weeks — Allen Edwards Salon WH · 10% off with Loyalty
  • Quick facial monthly — Beauty Skin Glow · Here She Glows-Skincare

Sometimes (quarterly or seasonal)

  • Full spa day — Tarzana Skin & Wellness · 5+ hours · ~$420
  • Couples treatment — Spa at Marriott Warner Center
  • Med-spa treatment (Botox, fillers, peels) — Epitome Med Spa
  • Pedicure + manicure full set — quarterly minimum

When you need it (sporadic, situational)

  • Postpartum recovery — Mom & Baby package · Tarzana Skin & Wellness
  • Pre-wedding glow-up — Bridal package, 6 sessions over 4 weeks
  • Stress recovery (post-deadline) — Cryohealthcare WH · 30 min · $60
  • Injury recovery — BASE Training & Physical Therapy
  • Mental wellness — Tarzana Treatment Centers (referrals only)
