Accessibility statement

Our commitment to digital access for every member of the West Valley.

The West Valley · Warner Center Chamber of Commerce is committed to ensuring digital accessibility for people with disabilities. We are continually improving the user experience for everyone and applying the relevant accessibility standards.

Conformance status

The Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) defines requirements for designers and developers to improve accessibility for people with disabilities. It defines three levels of conformance: Level A, Level AA, and Level AAA.

This website targets WCAG 2.1 Level AA conformance. The site is built to meet or exceed those requirements. We also support compliance with applicable provisions of the Americans with Disabilities Act (ADA) and Section 508 of the Rehabilitation Act.

Built-in accessibility tools

Look for the accessibility button (♿) in the bottom-left corner of any page. The on-site accessibility menu lets you adjust:

  • Text size — four levels from default to extra-large
  • Line height, letter spacing, and word spacing — for easier reading
  • Contrast modes — high contrast, dark mode, or full inversion
  • Saturation — reduce, increase, or convert to monochrome
  • Highlight links and headings — for clearer page scanning
  • Dyslexia-friendly font — easier letterforms for some readers
  • Larger cursor — for low-vision users
  • Pause animations — for users with vestibular sensitivities
  • Reading guide — a horizontal line that tracks your cursor
  • Hide images — text-only mode

Settings are saved on your device and re-apply automatically whenever you visit.

Compatibility with assistive technologies

The site is designed to be compatible with the following assistive technologies:

  • Screen readers — including JAWS, NVDA, VoiceOver (macOS / iOS), and TalkBack (Android)
  • Voice control software — Dragon NaturallySpeaking, Voice Control (macOS / iOS), Voice Access (Android)
  • Browser zoom up to 400% without loss of content or functionality
  • Browser-level high-contrast and reduced-motion settings (we respect prefers-contrast and prefers-reduced-motion media queries)
  • Keyboard-only navigation throughout

Standards we follow

  • WCAG 2.1 Level AA
  • Section 508 of the Rehabilitation Act
  • Americans with Disabilities Act (ADA) Title III
  • Unruh Civil Rights Act (California)

Multilingual access

Our content and AI Concierge are available in five written languages — English, Spanish, Russian, Armenian, and Chinese — with voice support for an additional three (Vietnamese, Japanese, Ukrainian). Use the language switcher (🌐) in the top header to change languages at any time.

Known limitations

Despite our best efforts, some areas of the site may not yet be fully accessible. Specifically:

  • Some embedded third-party widgets (the AI Concierge voice widget) may not always meet AA contrast on every theme combination. We are working with the vendor to improve this.
  • Member-uploaded content (logos, profile photos) may lack alt text in some cases. We provide member-side tools to add alternative text and remind members at upload.
  • Some PDF documents in our archive may not yet be fully tagged. We are reformatting these on an ongoing basis. If you need an accessible version of a specific document, contact us and we will provide it within two business days.
