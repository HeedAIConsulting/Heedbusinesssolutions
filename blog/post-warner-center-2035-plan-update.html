The Warner Center 2035 plan, four years in: what's actually built

Four years into the 2035 specific plan, Warner Center has added 1,400 housing units, 320,000 square feet of office, and one major park.

By The Chamber Team·Monday, November 10, 2025

The Warner Center 2035 plan was approved with a goal of transforming the area from office park to live-work-play district. Halfway through, it's mostly working.

Four years into the plan, Warner Center has added 1,400 new housing units (including the 220-unit Westgate apartments on Variel and the 380-unit Wisteria), 320,000 square feet of new office space, and one major public park (the 5.5-acre Promenade Park, opened 2024). Three more residential projects are under construction. The transformation is real, but it's also slower and more contested than the plan envisioned.

What's working

  • Residential demand exceeds projections — every new building has leased above pro-forma
  • Promenade Park has become the anchor for the chamber's May Day festival and Halloween Spooktacular
  • Office vacancy is down to 11% (county avg: 16%) — Warner Center has clawed back since 2023

What's lagging

  • The "complete streets" overlay has been politicized at city hall
  • Retail leasing is uneven — high turnover at Westfield Topanga's outer ring
  • The promised Topanga-line transit upgrade is still in study phase

The chamber convenes a monthly Warner Center stakeholders forum. It's open to all chamber members and the next session is the third Tuesday at 4pm at the Marriott.
