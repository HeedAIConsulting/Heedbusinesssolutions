Introducing the Chamber AI Concierge — your 24/7 guide to the West Valley

By Heed Business Solutions · Apr 30, 2026 · 3 min read

🤖

For nearly a century, the West Valley ~ Warner Center Chamber of Commerce has connected residents to the businesses that make this Valley home. Today, we're doing it with AI.

The Chamber AI Concierge is now live across every page of WVWCC.org. It's an always-on, plain-English guide to every member business, every Chamber event, and every community resource. No more digging through menus. No more wondering if a search will return the answer. Just ask, and the Concierge responds.

What it can do

  • Find a business. "Recommend a plumber that's a Chamber member" — get vetted, member-rated answers in seconds.
  • Plan an outing. "Date-night restaurant under $80 in Tarzana with a patio" — instant, filtered recommendations.
  • Plug into events. "What's the Chamber doing this week?" — full calendar, RSVP-ready.
  • Get unstuck. "I just moved to Reseda — set me up" — schools, doctors, utilities, the works.
  • Find resources. "Best after-school art classes for an 8-year-old" — searches our Parent Resource Guide instantly.

How it knows what to recommend

The Concierge is trained on the Chamber's own member directory, events database, and eight curated resource guides. Recommendations always prioritize Premium Sponsors, then Members, then Friends-of-the-Chamber. Non-member businesses appear only when no member fits the request — and those moments are flagged to our recruiting committee as outreach opportunities.

"The Concierge is the most member-value-creating thing the Chamber has launched in decades. Every recommendation it makes is a tracked lead — and members can see exactly who clicked, called, or booked."

Free for the community. Built for our members.

The Concierge is free for everyone — residents, visitors, prospective members. There's no login. There's no signup. It just works. For our members, the Concierge is the most powerful free lead-generation channel the Chamber has ever offered. Premium-tier members appear with priority placement and full lead attribution.
