Tarzana Treatment Centers turns 40

Four decades of behavioral health care on De Soto, with a renewed focus on adolescent mental health.

By The Chamber Team·Wednesday, March 4, 2026

Tarzana Treatment Centers has quietly become one of the largest behavioral-health providers in California, with its main campus on De Soto Avenue serving over 12,000 patients a year.

Founded in 1986 by a group of community physicians, TTC was built around a simple thesis: addiction and mental illness are medical conditions, not moral failings. Forty years in, that thesis has built a network of 14 facilities across LA County, with the De Soto flagship as the heart of the operation.

Why this matters now

The 40th anniversary expansion includes a new 60-bed adolescent mental-health unit — the first of its kind in the West Valley — and a partnership with LAUSD to bring crisis-trained counselors into Reseda and Cleveland high schools. Diana Williams (CEO of the chamber) sits on the advisory board.

TTC is hosting an open house Friday, March 13 from 9am-12pm. Tours, light food, and conversations with their clinical team. RSVP through the chamber events page.
