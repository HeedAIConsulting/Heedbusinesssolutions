Where Valley coffee snobs actually drink in 2026

Real chamber-member coffee shops, ranked by people who order their pour-over at 195°F.

By The Chamber Team·Saturday, November 29, 2025

Coffee is the most-consumed-and-least-discussed thing in the West Valley. Here are the chamber-member shops that take it seriously.

For the pour-over crowd

Brewstirs in Reseda has the only proper hand-brew bar this side of the 405. Owner Marco Chen sources beans from three roasters, alternates weekly, posts tasting notes on his Instagram. Member tier chamber member.

For the espresso enthusiasts

Caffe Etc. on Ventura has run a La Marzocco GS3 at 9 bars for 11 years. The cortado is the move.

For the work-from-coffee crowd

The Drift Coffee Bar at Warner Center Marriott — outlets, fast wifi, surprisingly good drip. No purchase required if you're a hotel guest, but they're lenient on chamber-member badges.

Honorable mentions (chamber-member but more food than coffee-focused)

  • Lemonade Canoga
  • Mel's Drive-In
  • Habit Cafe Reseda
