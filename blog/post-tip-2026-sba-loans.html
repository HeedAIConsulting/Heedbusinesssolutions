2026 SBA loan changes that affect Valley small businesses

Three SBA program updates from the 2025 reauthorization that change the math for chamber members.

By The Chamber Team·Wednesday, March 4, 2026

The 2025 SBA reauthorization brought three meaningful changes that affect chamber-member small businesses applying for federal loans in 2026.

1. SBA 7(a) loan limit raised to $7.5M

Previously $5M. The new limit primarily benefits acquisition-financing for chamber-member businesses transitioning ownership (a common scenario for our 30+ multigenerational members).

2. Express loans up to $750K (was $500K)

Faster turnaround, less paperwork. Chamber-member CPAs at the recent compliance breakfast called this the most useful change for working-capital lines.

3. New community-advantage SBLC pilot

New mission-driven lenders can now make SBA-guaranteed loans up to $350K to small businesses in opportunity zones. Reseda Blvd corridor qualifies — chamber members along it should evaluate.
