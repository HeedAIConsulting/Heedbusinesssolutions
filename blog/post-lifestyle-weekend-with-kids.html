A perfect Saturday in the West Valley with kids under 10

A 9am-to-5pm itinerary that won't exhaust your kids or your wallet.

By The Chamber Team·Tuesday, February 17, 2026

Most "things to do with kids" articles fall into two camps: paid attractions ($300 for a family of four) or "go to the park." Here's a real Saturday that costs about $80 and will exhaust your kids in the right way.

9:00am — Lemonade for breakfast

Canoga Avenue location. Kid-friendly seating, healthy options, and the chalk wall keeps the under-5s busy. ~$30 for a family of four.

10:30am — Tarzana Recreation Center playground

Recently re-rubbered surface, 4-12 age range, decent shade. Free.

12:30pm — Pizza Rev or Stonefire on Ventura

Stonefire is a Tarzana institution. Pizza Rev is faster. Either works. ~$45.

2:00pm — Westfield Topanga food court walk

Hit the kids' carousel ($3), browse the Lego store, sit on the indoor benches near the fountain, sneak a Wetzel's. Free if you're strict; $10 if you're not.

3:30pm — Library hour at the Tarzana branch

The Tarzana branch of LAPL has the best kids' section in the West Valley. Free WiFi for parents. Story time most Saturdays.

5:00pm — Home, exhausted

For dinner, just order from a chamber-member restaurant. You've done enough.
