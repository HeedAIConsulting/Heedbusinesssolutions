Square vs Stripe in 2026: which one fits your Valley business?

A clear-headed comparison with no affiliate links and no hype.

By The Chamber Team·Tuesday, December 30, 2025

About one-third of chamber-member businesses ask the same question every quarter: "should I be on Square or Stripe?" Here's the answer, depending on the kind of business you run.

Square is better if you:

  • Have a physical retail or restaurant location
  • Need integrated POS hardware
  • Want appointment booking included (Square Appointments)
  • Need same-day deposits
  • Don't want to think about implementation

Stripe is better if you:

  • Run an online-first business
  • Have a developer (or use a no-code tool that integrates Stripe)
  • Need international payments
  • Want lower fees at scale (1% – 2.5% lower above $250K/year)
  • Need recurring billing with complex rules

The truth most articles won't tell you

Most West Valley chamber-member small businesses (under $1M GMV) save more time with Square than they save in fees with Stripe. The hour you don't spend on integration is worth the 0.4% fee difference.

The chamber accepts both for memberships. The new automated onboarding flow uses Square.
