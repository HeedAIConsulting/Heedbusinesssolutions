Pitch a Guest Post

Members and local experts welcome. We publish ~6 guest posts per month and amplify them across our newsletter and social channels.

Submit your pitch

Editor reviews submissions weekly. We'll respond within 7 business days.

What we publish

  • Practical advice from Valley business owners
  • Industry insights with West Valley relevance
  • Member stories told well
  • Civic/community-focused commentary
  • Original analysis and research

What we don't

  • Pure promotional pieces (use Member Deals instead)
  • Republished content from elsewhere
  • Political endorsements
  • Anything off-topic from the West Valley
💡
Tip from our editor

A specific, narrow story always beats a broad one. "How we went from 0 to 200 wedding bookings in our first year" wins over "Tips for marketing a wedding business."
