Tarzana Family Dental — three generations under one roof

A multigenerational dental practice on Ventura that handles grandparents, parents, and grandchildren in the same week.

By The Chamber Team·Tuesday, December 2, 2025

Tarzana Family Dental has built a practice on a fairly unusual claim: more than half their patient households include three generations.

Dr. Karim Mehrabian opened the practice in 1998. His daughter Dr. Nadia joined in 2018. Their patient files now span everything from first cleanings for kids whose grandparents were also patients here, to complex prosthodontics for those same grandparents.

What they do well

  • Pediatric dentistry with kid-friendly Saturday hours
  • Cosmetic and restorative work
  • Sleep-apnea oral appliances
  • Medicare and most major insurance accepted
  • Free annual cleaning for new chamber members (mention code WVCC)

Member tier Chamber member. 18737 Ventura Blvd, Tarzana.
