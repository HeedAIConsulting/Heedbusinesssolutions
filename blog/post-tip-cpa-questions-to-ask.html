8 questions to ask before hiring a CPA in 2026

Beyond price and pedigree — what actually predicts whether a CPA will do right by your Valley small business.

By The Chamber Team·Sunday, April 12, 2026

CPAs are not interchangeable. Eight chamber-member CPAs contributed to this list of the questions you should be asking before you hire one.

The eight questions

  • 1. How many businesses in my industry/size do you currently serve?
  • 2. Will I work with you, a junior, or a rotating team?
  • 3. Do you proactively reach out about tax-planning opportunities, or do I have to ask?
  • 4. What's your turnaround on basic questions during the year (not tax season)?
  • 5. What software stack do you use and is it compatible with my POS / payroll / banking?
  • 6. Do you do bookkeeping in-house or do you require a separate provider?
  • 7. What's your fee structure — fixed, hourly, value-based?
  • 8. Will you represent me to the IRS or only prepare returns?
