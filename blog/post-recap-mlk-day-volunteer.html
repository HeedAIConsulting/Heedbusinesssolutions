Recap: MLK Day chamber volunteer drive

160 chamber-member volunteers, four worksites, one Monday.

By The Chamber Team·Monday, January 26, 2026

The chamber's annual MLK Day of Service drew 160 volunteers across four West Valley worksites this year — our biggest turnout yet for a one-day event.

Volunteer worksites: the Boys & Girls Club West Valley clubhouse refresh, Tarzana Treatment Centers food-pantry restock, the Reseda Library reading-garden cleanup, and a Habitat-for-Humanity build in Northridge. Felicia coordinated the rosters; chamber-member businesses sponsored breakfast and lunch at all four sites.

By the numbers

  • 160 volunteers
  • 520 cumulative service hours
  • 12 chamber-member businesses sponsored meals
  • 3 nonprofit beneficiary organizations

Save the date: MLK Day 2027 is Monday, January 18. Sign-ups will open in November.
