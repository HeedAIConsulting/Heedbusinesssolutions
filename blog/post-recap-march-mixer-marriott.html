Recap: March mixer at Warner Center Marriott

Allegro at the Marriott hosted, the AI for Business circle launched, and 110 members showed up.

By The Chamber Team·Saturday, April 4, 2026

The March chamber mixer was hosted by Allegro at the Warner Center Marriott — and it doubled as the official launch of the new AI for Business Connection Circle.

110 members. Open bar. The new chef's passed-canapés (the duck confit on potato cake was the unanimous favorite). And about 25 minutes into the mixer, Diana welcomed Michael Bowers (Heed AI) to the podium to formally launch the AI for Business Connection Circle, which now meets the first Wednesday of each month at the Marriott.

New connections

Three new chamber-member businesses signed up at the mixer. Two existing members upgraded their tier. One ribbon cutting was scheduled for May.

April mixer is at Belmont Village Calabasas — 4/29, 5:30pm.
