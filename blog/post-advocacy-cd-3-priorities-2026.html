CD-3 priorities for 2026 — what we're asking Councilmember Blumenfield for

The chamber's formal advocacy agenda for Council District 3, presented at the December board meeting.

By The Chamber Team·Wednesday, January 7, 2026

Each January the chamber publishes its formal advocacy agenda — the items we'll spend chamber time and political capital on with Councilmember Blumenfield, the planning commissions, and Sacramento.

Top three for 2026

  • Sherman Way streetscape (already secured — see April 15 post)
  • Topanga-Reseda corridor traffic mitigation — particularly the bottleneck at Ventura
  • Property tax abatement for new small-business buildouts in Reseda Opportunity Zone

Watching closely

  • Warner Center 2035 plan amendments
  • LAUSD's school-attendance-boundary review (impacts Cleveland and Reseda HS)
  • State sick-leave expansion — small-business compliance impacts
  • LADWP commercial water-rate proposal

The chamber's advocacy committee meets the second Wednesday of each month. Open to all chamber members. Mark Cudacua chairs.
