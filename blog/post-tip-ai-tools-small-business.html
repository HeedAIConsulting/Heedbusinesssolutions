Five AI tools that actually save Valley small businesses time

No hype. Just five AI tools chamber members are using right now to save 5–10 hours per week.

By The Chamber Team·Tuesday, November 25, 2025

AI is having a moment, but most "AI for small business" articles are written by people who have never run one. Here are five tools — used by actual West Valley chamber members — that are saving them real hours every week.

1. Otter.ai for meeting notes

Tim Gaspar Insurance uses Otter on every client call. Auto-transcribes, summarizes action items, and sends them to the client. Cost: $20/month. Time saved: 4 hours/week.

2. Claude or ChatGPT for first drafts

Several member CPAs use Claude to draft client emails — explanations of tax positions, payment-plan letters, organizer reminders. The CPA reviews, edits, sends. The first-draft time goes from 25 minutes to 5.

3. Apollo or ZoomInfo for prospect research

For the BD-driven members in real estate, financial planning, and B2B services: 30 minutes of prospect research compresses to 5. The chamber maintains a member discount with Apollo through the Valley Biz Brief partnership.

4. Bench or Pilot for bookkeeping

Several chamber-member small businesses have moved off "spouse handles QuickBooks" to AI-augmented bookkeeping. Spouse keeps their day job. Books are cleaner. Tax season is shorter.

5. The chamber's own AI Concierge

Free for residents and members. When customers ask "where can I get X in the West Valley?" — they get answers that include your business by name. Without you doing anything.
