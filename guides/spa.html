Med-Spas vs Day Spas — How to choose

If you're new to the wellness scene, the distinction matters. Day spas are pure relaxation. Medspas are physician-supervised — Botox, fillers, IPL, microneedling. Most West Valley addresses are a hybrid these days.

Local Tips From the Wellness Committee

🅿️ Where to actually park

Ventura Blvd metered spots free after 6pm and Sundays. The lot behind 18345 Ventura validates two hours.

📅 Member-Sunday discounts

Half a dozen member spas run Chamber-only Sunday rates — present member ID. Best deals at Topanga Day Spa and Sage Wellness.

💳 HSA/FSA-eligible

Massage, cryo, IV drips with a doctor's note are all HSA/FSA-eligible. Ask the front desk for an itemized receipt.

👯 Friend-and-family rate

Most member spas offer 20% off when you book two services back-to-back. Bring a friend, split the savings.

🎁 Gifting tip

Burke Williams day-pass + 50-min massage is the perfect $200 thank-you gift. Tax-deductible if it's a vendor relationship.

🏃 Athletes' loop

Cryo at 8am, red-light at 8:30, IV drip at 9 — all in one stop at Cryohealthcare. Out by 10. Members do this every Saturday.

Wellness FAQ

What's the difference between a day spa and a medspa?

Day spas offer relaxation services — massage, facials, body treatments. Medspas are physician-supervised and offer medical-grade procedures: Botox, fillers, laser, microneedling. Many West Valley locations blend both.

Best couples massage in the West Valley?

Serenity Valley Spa (Tarzana) has the only private hammam-style couples suite. Burke Williams Woodland Hills offers a side-by-side suite with a fireplace. Both take same-week appointments.

Where can I get cryotherapy in Woodland Hills?

Cryohealthcare West Valley on Topanga Canyon Blvd. Whole-body, localized, red-light, infrared sauna, contrast plunges. Chamber-member intro: $39.

Postpartum massage near Tarzana?

Serenity Valley's "Mom & Baby Recovery" program — abdominal binding, lymphatic drainage, lactation-friendly draping. Available 2 weeks postpartum onward. HSA/FSA accepted.

Does the Chamber discount apply to spas?

Yes. Most members offer 10–20% off first visits, plus standing Chamber-family discounts. Mention "WVWCCC" at booking.

Are bridal spa packages available?

Yes — Burke Williams, Serenity Valley, and Aida Med Spa all run bridal-party packages with champagne, blowouts, mani/pedi. Book 4–6 weeks ahead for Saturdays.
