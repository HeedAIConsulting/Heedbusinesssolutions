Veteran-Parent Hacks

🚗 Skip the 5pm traffic

Westfield Topanga is gridlocked 4:30-6 weekends. Hit it at 11am or after 7pm.

📦 Pack the "park bag"

Sunscreen, snacks, towels, change of clothes, $20 cash, two water bottles. Keep in the trunk.

🎟️ Universal Annual Pass math

Pays off at ~3 visits/year. Resident-payment-plan splits the cost over 12 months.

🎂 Mid-week birthday savings

Color Me Mine and KinderGym both run weekday party rates 30% below Saturdays.

🍦 Magpies happy hour

BOGO scoops Wed 5-7pm. Family of four under $14.

🎪 Splash & dine combo

Reseda Park splash → Mama's Pizza Garden walking distance. Park-day perfection.

Family Activities FAQ

What free things to do with kids in the Valley?

Reseda Park splash pad (May-Oct), Lake Balboa pedal boats, Tarzana library story-time, Skirball first Thursdays, Westfield Village fountain, Warner Park concerts, free family swim Sat 12-2 at Reseda Park.

Where to throw a 6-year-old's birthday in Woodland Hills?

Color Me Mine Tarzana, KinderGym Kids' party room, Sky Zone Reseda, Winnetka Bowl, Pretend City Encino. Saturdays book 4-6 weeks ahead.

Best summer camps in 2026?

iD Tech at CSUN, Boys & Girls Club West Valley, Calabasas Tennis, Theater of Arts, JCC Maccabi Sports. Most open registration in February.

Indoor play for rainy days?

Sky Zone Reseda, KinderGym Kids, Pretend City Encino, Skirball Noah's Ark, Tarzana branch library kids' room.

Where to see fireworks July 4th?

Warner Center's Fourth of July Spectacular at 21300 Erwin St (free), Calabasas Country Club show, Pierce College show. Arrive by 6pm.
