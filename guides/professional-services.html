How to Hire a Pro

📞 Always 3 calls

Even on a $300 task. Chamber-member pros expect this; the conversation reveals fit better than the quote.

🤝 Verify the license

CA Bar number, CSLB, DRE — every license is publicly searchable. We list license #s on Premium listings.

📅 Annual check-in

Once a year, your CPA + attorney + insurance broker should sit down with you for 60 min. Chamber hosts free Q4 group meetings.

⏱️ 24-hr response test

If they don't return your initial call in one business day, they won't return your urgent call either. Move on.

📑 Engagement letters

Always required. If they don't send one, don't proceed. Real pros never skip this.

💼 Specialty matters

A great divorce attorney is a mediocre business attorney. Hire the specialty, not the brand.

Pro-Services FAQ

Best estate-planning attorney in Woodland Hills?

Topanga Estate Planning is the most-referred Chamber-member firm — wills, living trusts, probate. Average fee for a basic family trust: $2,800-$4,500. Free 30-min consult for Chamber members.

How do I choose a CPA?

Three things: (1) Industry specialty? (2) Year-round availability? (3) Quarterly KPI dashboard? Warner Center CPA Group, Topanga CPA & Wealth, and Tarzana Bookkeeping all offer free Chamber consults.

Best real-estate agent in Tarzana?

West Valley Realty Group is the largest member firm by 91356 transactions. Compass Tarzana, Coldwell Banker, Rodeo Realty all have strong teams. Always interview 2-3.

Fee-only vs commission financial advisor?

Fee-only (CFP, fiduciary) is better for retirement & tax planning. Commission-based is fine for insurance/annuity needs. The Chamber's Wealth Roundtable lists which is which for every member firm.

Average cost of a basic LLC formation?

$1,200-$1,800 with a Chamber-member attorney including operating agreement, EIN, and S-corp election review. CA $800 franchise tax is separate.
