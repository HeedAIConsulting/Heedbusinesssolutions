Smart-Owner Tips

📅 April HVAC tune-up

Book before May 1. Wait and you're calling 8 companies in July, all booked 3 weeks out.

💧 Drain-line camera at year 5

Most Valley homes have orangeburg or clay sewer laterals. A $250 camera at year 5 of ownership saves $9k of slab surprise.

🌳 Tree trim before October winds

Schedule trims by mid-September. Insurance won't cover a tree fall caused by deferred trimming.

🪟 Window seal check

Sun-faced double-pane windows fail at 12-15 yrs. Hazy interior = seal failure. Replace one room at a time.

🔌 GFCI age

If your kitchen/bath GFCIs are older than 12 yrs, they're past their tested life. $35 swap, big safety win.

💰 Always 3 quotes

For anything over $2,000. Chamber-member pros expect this; the bid spread is your data.

Home-Owner FAQ

Who do I call for an emergency plumbing leak in Tarzana at 2am?

Valley Master Plumbing and West Valley Plumbing Co. both run 24/7 lines. Chamber-member rate: 10% off labor. Tap the Concierge button — we have the on-call list.

What's the average cost of a panel upgrade in Woodland Hills?

Standard 100A→200A is $2,800–$4,500 including LADWP permit. Add EV charger at same time: +$400-600 vs doing it later.

When should I service my AC?

April. Valley hits 95°F+ by mid-May; HVAC techs book out 3-4 weeks once it's hot. Cool Valley HVAC: $89 Chamber tune-up.

Do I need a permit for a kitchen remodel?

Yes if you're moving plumbing, electrical, or load-bearing walls. LA Building & Safety: 4-6 weeks. Chamber Remodel Committee has a permit-expediter list.

How do I find a vetted handyman?

Every handyman here is licensed (or under the $500 unlicensed-work threshold), insured, and Chamber-vetted. Mr. Fix-It WH and Tarzana Handyman both hold C-61 licenses.

What's a fair tree-trimming rate?

$350-$700 per medium tree (under 30 ft) including haul-away. Drought-stressed eucalyptus: $800-$1,400.
