Local tips

The little things that make the Valley work

Best parking near Westfield Topanga (without paying)

The two-hour free street spots on Erwin and Califa fill by 11am on Saturdays. Pro tip: take Costco's lot if you've got a Costco membership — first 90 minutes are validated. The library lot on Owensmouth is the secret weapon for evenings (free after 6pm).

Where to take out-of-town visitors who say "show me LA"

Skip Hollywood. Start at the Sherman Oaks Galleria for an iconic view, brunch at Casa Vega in Sherman Oaks (Mexican since 1956 — your guests will think they discovered something), then drive Mulholland to the Skirball overlook for the city panorama. Top it with dinner at Fogo de Chão in Warner Center. They'll never forget it.

Kid-friendly happy hours (real ones)

The Drift Inn at the Warner Center Marriott has a kid menu and quiet outdoor seating during weekday happy hour. The Lemonade on Canoga has a chalk wall and outdoor lawn. Yard House in Topanga has crayons, a sports-bar volume that masks toddler noise, and a 20-tap kids' juice/soda menu.

The under-known Reseda food gems

The strip mall on Reseda Blvd between Saticoy and Vanowen is one of the densest hyper-local food clusters in the city. Don't skip El Burrito Express, the Korean fried chicken at Daily Bites, or the third-generation deli at Joe's Bagels. Most are chamber members. All are cash-friendly.

The fastest way to get a vetted home pro on a weekend

Open the AI Concierge, type "I need a plumber Saturday morning, water leak." It'll surface 3 chamber-member plumbers with weekend hours, ranked by proximity, with phone numbers and online booking links. From "oh no" to "scheduled" in under 60 seconds.
