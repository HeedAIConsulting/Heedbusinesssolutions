🤝 Connection Circles

Business Owners' FAQ

Best CPA for a small West Valley LLC?

Warner Center CPA Group handles most Chamber-member LLCs; QuickBooks-certified, year-round bookkeeping. Tarzana Bookkeeping is the best entry-tier option ($350-650/mo).

Do I need a business attorney to form an LLC?

Not strictly — but a 30-min consult catches operating-agreement and tax-election mistakes that cost thousands later. Valley Business Law Group offers Chamber members a free 30-min consult.

Fair monthly fee for managed IT?

$95-$150/seat/mo for full managed IT in the West Valley — monitoring, patching, helpdesk, basic cyber. SecureValley MSP and The Computer Doctor are both members.

How do I find commercial space in Warner Center?

CBRE Warner Center, Marcus & Millichap, West Valley Realty Commercial all run live availability lists. Coworking via Spaces (Westfield) and Premier Workspaces (Warner Center).

Best business banking option locally?

Bank of the Sierra, City National, and US Bank all have West Valley business bankers. Bank of the Sierra has strongest SBA-7(a) lending. Mention Chamber for waived account-open fees.
