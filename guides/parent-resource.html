How to navigate Regional Center funding

North LA County Regional Center serves the 818. The intake process takes 60–90 days. We strongly recommend starting before your child turns 3 if there are any developmental concerns — providers we list above are contracted and can help with paperwork. Free intake at nlacrc.org.

Local Tips Every Valley Parent Should Know

🚗 The 5pm school-pickup move

If your kid does Boys & Girls Club at Reseda Park, take Lindley north — Reseda Blvd is gridlocked between 4:30 and 5:45.

🍎 Free family swim

Reseda Park pool runs free family-swim hours Saturday 12–2pm in summer. Bring snacks; parking fills by noon.

📚 Library card hack

The Tarzana branch has the LAPL's best kids' room. Story time Wed 10am, baby Mondays. Free Lynda + Brainfuse with card.

🎟️ Free museum day

Skirball is free first Thursdays after 4pm. Bring strollers — the Noah's Ark gallery is the best toddler hour in LA.

🍦 Best ice-cream stop

Magpies on Ventura at Reseda Blvd. Vegan options, real cones. Wednesday is locals' family-rate night.

👶 Last-minute babysitter

Chamber Connection-Circles families share a vetted-sitter Slack — ask the front desk for the invite link.

Parent FAQ

What is the best preschool in Tarzana?

Sunrise Children's Academy on Ventura Blvd is the most-requested Reggio-inspired preschool in Tarzana. Valley Montessori, Little Sprouts Co-op, and Stepping Stones are also strong picks. Sunrise has rolling enrollment; the others have waitlists in spring.

When should I start touring preschools?

Most West Valley preschools open tour calendars in October for the following September. Competitive ones (Calvary, Westmark, Bridges) require apps by early January. Co-ops accept rolling, but spots fill by June.

Where is the best pediatrician in Woodland Hills?

Providence Tarzana Children's Health Center, Kaiser Permanente Woodland Hills, and Pediatric Group of Tarzana are the three largest networks. UCLA Health's Reseda outpost handles complex specialties.

Good first-birthday venue?

For ages 1–3: Color Me Mine, KinderGym party room, and Boys & Girls Club splash deck. For 4–7: Winnetka Bowl, Sky Zone, Pretend City Encino.

Are there special-needs resources locally?

Yes. Help Group (Westmark, Summit View), Center for Discovery Tarzana, ABA Solutions, Reseda Speech, and Pediatric Therapy Network. North LA County Regional Center funds many.

Chamber-member family discount?

Most listings here offer 5–15% off intro classes, first-visit fees, or party packages for Chamber-member families. Show your member ID or mention "WVWCCC" at booking.
