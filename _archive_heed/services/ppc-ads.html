How It Works

From Audit to Optimized Campaigns

A systematic approach that eliminates guesswork and builds paid advertising programs engineered around your specific revenue goals.

01

Account Audit

We begin by reviewing your current ad accounts (if any), understanding your goals, competitive landscape, and ideal customer profile. For new accounts, this is a discovery phase that establishes the baseline for everything we build.

02

Campaign Architecture

We build the full campaign structure — account settings, ad groups, audiences, targeting parameters, negative keywords, and bid strategies — designed around your specific conversion goals and budget constraints.

03

Creative Development

Ad copy and creative assets are written, designed, and reviewed before anything goes live. We build multiple variations for testing so we can identify winning messages quickly and scale what works.

04

Launch & Optimize

Campaigns go live with conversion tracking fully verified. We monitor performance daily, make adjustments as data accumulates, and send you a plain-language weekly report that shows exactly what your ad spend is producing.
