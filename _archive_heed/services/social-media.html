How It Works

From Strategy to Scroll-Stopping Content

A structured onboarding process that gets us fully aligned on your brand voice and content direction — so everything we publish feels authentically yours.

01

Audit & Strategy

We review your existing social presence, analyze competitor accounts, and define your content strategy — platform priorities, content pillars, brand voice guidelines, and audience growth targets — before a single post goes out.

02

Content Calendar

A full month of content is planned in advance, organized into a shared content calendar for your review. You see every post before it publishes and can request changes — you stay in control without having to do the work yourself.

03

Creative Production

Graphics, captions, hashtags, and video assets are produced for every post on the calendar. Reels and stories are scripted, edited, and formatted for each platform's native specifications so they perform at their best.

04

Publish & Engage

Content is scheduled and published on optimal days and times. We monitor comments and messages, engage with your community on your behalf, and send a monthly report covering reach, engagement, follower growth, and next-month priorities.
