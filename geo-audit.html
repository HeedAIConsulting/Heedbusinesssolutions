What Is GEO

Generative Engine Optimization Is the Next Evolution of Search

Traditional SEO optimizes for link-based rankings. GEO optimizes for how AI models interpret and surface your information in conversational answers. If your content is not structured for AI citation, you will not be recommended, no matter how well you rank on page one.

AI Engines Summarize, Not Link

ChatGPT and Google AI Overviews generate answers directly. If your content is not structured for extraction, you get skipped even when you have the best answer.

Structured Data Is the New Currency

Schema markup, FAQ pages, entity definitions, and speakable content tell AI engines exactly who you are, what you do, and why you should be cited.

Authority Over Volume

AI engines prefer authoritative, specific, well-structured content over high-volume generic pages. Quality and clarity win over keyword stuffing.

Your Competitors Are Already Moving

Firms that optimize for GEO now will own the citation space for their industry. Waiting means ceding that ground to firms that move first.

12-Point Assessment

What the GEO Readiness Audit Evaluates

We evaluate the 12 factors that determine whether AI-powered search engines can accurately understand, cite, and recommend your business.

Structured Data Quality

Is your schema markup present, valid, and comprehensive enough for AI engines to extract key business information?

Entity Definition Clarity

Can AI engines clearly identify who you are, what you do, where you operate, and who you serve within the first paragraph of key pages?

FAQ Coverage and Format

Do your pages include well-structured Q&A content with FAQPage schema that AI engines can directly extract and cite?

Content Authority Signals

Does your content demonstrate expertise, experience, authoritativeness, and trustworthiness (E-E-A-T) in ways AI models can detect?

Speakable Content Designation

Have you marked content as speakable so voice assistants and AI tools can read key sections aloud when answering questions?

Local Business Markup

Is your location, service area, and contact information structured so AI engines can recommend you for local queries?

Service Description Specificity

Are your services described with enough specificity that AI can match you to precise client queries rather than generic categories?

Citation Readiness

Does your content contain clear, declarative statements that AI engines can directly quote when answering user questions?

Mobile and Performance

AI engines factor in page speed and mobile experience. Slow or broken mobile pages reduce your authority score.

Internal Linking Structure

Does your site create clear topical relationships between pages that help AI engines understand your full scope of expertise?

Content Freshness Signals

AI engines prefer current information. Are your pages dated, updated regularly, and signaling active expertise?

Competitive GEO Positioning

How do you compare to similar firms in your market? We benchmark your GEO readiness against competitors in your industry and geography.

Frequently Asked Questions

Common Questions About GEO and the Readiness Audit

Generative Engine Optimization (GEO) is the practice of structuring your website content, metadata, and schema markup so that AI-powered search engines like ChatGPT, Google AI Overviews, Perplexity, and Claude can accurately understand, cite, and recommend your business. Unlike traditional SEO, which optimizes for link-based rankings, GEO optimizes for how AI models interpret and surface your information in conversational answers.

More than 40% of online searches now involve AI-generated answers. If your website is not structured for generative engines, your business may not appear when potential clients ask AI tools for recommendations. This is especially critical for service professionals like attorneys, financial advisors, and consultants, where trust and authority determine which businesses get cited.

The audit evaluates 12 key areas: structured data and schema markup quality, entity definition clarity, FAQ coverage and format, content authority signals, speakable content designation, local business markup accuracy, service description specificity, citation readiness, mobile and performance scores, internal linking structure, content freshness signals, and competitive GEO positioning against similar firms in your market.
