About Heed Business Solutions

We Engineer How High-Value Clients Experience Your Firm

Heed Business Solutions is a Los Angeles consulting firm that helps service professionals attract, convert, and retain high-value clients by redesigning how their business is perceived, positioned, and experienced from first contact to long-term relationship.

The Principal

Michael Bowers

Michael Bowers has spent 25 years helping service-based businesses in Los Angeles build the systems, presence, and client experience required to win high-value relationships. His work spans estate attorneys, entertainment companies, government agencies, real estate developers, and technology firms.

His approach starts with a simple question: when a high-value prospect evaluates your firm, what do they actually experience? From the first Google result to the intake call to the onboarding process, every touchpoint either builds trust or introduces doubt. Michael engineers each one for precision.

Today, Heed Business Solutions focuses on service firms that serve or want to serve selective, high-net-worth clients. We combine custom web development, AI-powered intake systems, CRM workflows, and strategic positioning into a single, integrated system that converts the right clients and compounds over time.

  • 25 Years in Business Active since 2000, serving the Los Angeles market across every major sector
  • Legal Industry Specialist Deep expertise in estate, probate, and asset protection attorney marketing and systems
  • AI and Automation Expert AI phone reception, CRM automation, and GEO optimization for professional services
  • 100+ Client Engagements From solo practitioners to government agencies, across 8+ industry verticals

Our Philosophy

Outcomes Over Tactics. Experience Over Marketing.

Sell Confidence, Not Services

We do not lead with websites, ads, or automation. We lead with the outcome: high-value clients who trust your firm, move forward quickly, and stay for the long term.

Engineer the Full Experience

Every touchpoint matters. From search result to intake call to onboarding, we design a consistent, high-trust experience that matches what selective clients expect.

Build Systems That Compound

One-off campaigns decay. We build retention and referral systems that generate compounding returns, so each client relationship creates the next one.

Service Firms That Serve High-Value Clients

Family Offices

Financial Advisors and Wealth Managers

Luxury Real Estate

Specialty Medical Practices

Insurance Brokers

Entertainment and Creative Firms

Professional Service Firms
