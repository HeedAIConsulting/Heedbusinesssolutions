Her Story

Your Neighbor. Your Advocate.

I first decided to become an attorney in 7th grade. I always felt a deep need to protect people — to make sure everything is fair and right in their lives. That drive has never left me.

My legal career began at the St. Petersburg District Attorney's Office in Russia in the late 1990s, where I earned a Bachelor's in Jurisprudence. I then moved to Los Angeles, mastered English while raising a newborn, earned my J.D. from the University of West Los Angeles, and passed the California Bar on my first attempt. My early career here included the Los Angeles City Attorney's Office and volunteer work at Public Counsel.

I am a mother, daughter, business owner, and community member. I have been through many of life's unexpected turns, and I understand how critical it is to have your affairs in order. My clients come from all walks of life, with different family dynamics and financial situations, and it is my honor to guide each of them toward becoming the heroes of their own families — people who took care of the people they love.

My personal mission is to educate people about the risks of not having an estate plan. I regularly present real life examples in community settings to help families understand why preparation matters.

Outside the office, I am a grandmother, mother of three, horse rider, and pet owner. The weight of uncertainty about the future takes a toll on daily life. When you are prepared, that weight lifts. My goal is to make sure every client can concentrate on what truly matters — their families — with complete peace of mind about their future.
