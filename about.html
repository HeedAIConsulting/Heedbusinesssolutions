Our story

Since 1930.

We were founded in 1930 by a handful of merchants who believed business and community grow strongest when they grow together. Almost a century later, we're still doing the work — and now, with AI on our side, we're doing more of it for more people.

Our work

What the Chamber does

🤝

Connect

200+ networking events a year. Connection Circles, monthly breakfasts, ribbon cuttings, mixers, and our flagship Food & Wine Event.

📣

Promote

Member directory, eight community resource guides, the AI Concierge, weekly newsletter (12,800+ subscribers), social channels, and member spotlights.

📚

Educate

Speaker breakfasts, Young Professionals network, Health & Wellness committee, Home Improvement Professionals network, and educational programs for member-business owners.

🌟

Give back

Adopt-a-School, scholarship fund, Small Business Disaster Relief, Senior Programs, and Community Choice Awards — all through the Chamber Foundation.
