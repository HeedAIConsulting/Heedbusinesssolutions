Webinar Recap: AI — Real-World Use Cases for Chambers

By Diana Williams · CEO & CBF Director · November 21, 2025

We know AI is a game-changer, but what does that look like for your organization? Our latest webinar discussed real-world, high-ROI AI applications being used by chamber executives today.

Our community has been asking what AI looks like in practice — past the buzz, past the hype, past the "we should probably do something" anxiety. So we sat down with chamber leaders from Texas, Arizona, and Tennessee who are already running AI inside their chambers, and asked them to show us the receipts.

Here's what stood out.

Learn how your peers are leveraging AI to:

Boost retention & revenue

The Longview, TX Chamber increased its first-year retention rate to a projected 55% and generated over $10,000 in passive membership sales through automated follow-up funnels. The play wasn't fancy: it was a sequence of 5 emails timed to onboarding milestones, drafted by AI, personalized per business vertical, and reviewed by staff in 10 minutes a week.

Secure funding

The Greater Phoenix Chamber Foundation is using AI to draft grant proposals (getting them 75% complete immediately) and to summarize detailed federal grant requirements down to a one-page brief their team can decide on in minutes instead of hours.

Save staff time

Automate board briefing podcasts and condense hours of advocacy video into concise executive summaries. The Knoxville Chamber turned a 90-minute board meeting recording into a 4-minute audio briefing for members on Monday morning. Members loved it. Staff loved it more.

Maximize communications

Optimize newsletters to avoid spam filters and see open rates surge from 35% to 57%. The trick: AI-driven subject-line testing, plain-text alternatives, and removing the "newsletter look" that triggers filters in the first place.

"This isn't science fiction. This is staff who used to have 6 hours of admin per week getting it down to 2, and using the other 4 hours to actually serve members."
