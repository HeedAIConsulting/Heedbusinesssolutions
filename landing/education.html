Common questions

Education FAQ

Is the Parent Resource Guide actually used by parents?

Yes — it's our most-trafficked guide. The 2025 print run hit 18,000 households, and the digital edition averages 4,200 unique parent users a month. PTA partners at Tarzana Elementary, Woodland Hills Charter, and El Camino Real send it home in their welcome packets. Members typically see Guide-driven inquiries starting in week two of August.

Can I be listed in multiple guides — Parent, Education, and Family Activities?

Yes, and we encourage it. A Mandarin tutor running summer camps is naturally a fit for all three, and the Concierge correlates the listings so parents see your full offering. Silver tier and above includes premium placement in all three at no additional cost.

Do you support TK–12 schools the same way as tutors?

Yes — but schools usually join at the Gold or Platinum tier because the open-house and admissions cycle benefits from the featured directory placement and the annual Education Forum sponsorship slot. We work with your admissions director to syndicate open-house dates across our PTA and HOA partners.

Is there a back-to-school promotion window?

Yes. Every August we publish a special "Back-to-School Issue" of the West Valley Weekly with member spotlights, sponsored sections for each grade band, and a print insert at our school partners. Members at Bronze and above get a guaranteed mention; Silver and above get a featured spotlight.

How does the Education Forum work?

It's our annual February event for parents, educators, and admissions directors — last year drew 380 attendees. Silver members get a complimentary booth, Gold get a 12-minute breakout session, Platinum sponsor the keynote. The Forum also publishes a take-home program guide, distributed in print and online for 90 days afterward.

Do you handle educator background-check verification?

We require LiveScan or equivalent for every individual instructor or tutor listed under a member, and display a verified-fingerprinted badge on the profile. It is the single biggest trust signal for parents and we will not list without it. If you don't have it, we can connect you to a low-cost LiveScan partner.
