Common questions

Spa & Beauty FAQ

I run a med-spa with injectables — does anything in your guide require medical oversight to publish?

Your medical-director NPI and CA Board of Pharmacy compounding partner (if applicable) are listed on your profile so consumers can verify. We don't require it to join, but med-spas with verified medical oversight get a verified badge that converts measurably better — our internal data shows roughly 2.4x the booking conversion versus unverified listings.

How does the Spa & Wellness Guide actually drive bookings?

The Guide is published online and in our quarterly print insert at 22 partner locations (hospitals, hotels, HOA newsletters). Each member gets a tap-to-book link, gallery, and seasonal offer slot. Members report 8–22 new client bookings per quarter from the Guide alone, with the strongest months around Mother's Day, back-to-school, and December.

Can I list multiple locations under one membership?

One membership covers up to two locations within the West Valley footprint at the Bronze tier and above. Three or more locations move to a Silver multi-location membership at $1,495/yr — still cheaper than two separate listings, with combined social features and a shared Concierge entry that surfaces the closest location to the resident.

Are licensed-only services (lashes, microblading, injectables) handled differently?

Yes. We require a current CA license number for every licensed service listed, and we display the license type next to the service. Residents trust this; the Concierge filters by it. We re-verify annually as part of renewal.

What about cross-promotion with other beauty businesses?

Two ways. The Spa & Wellness Connection Circle meets monthly (capped at 12 members, no competing specialties at the same meeting). And the Chamber runs an annual "Glow Up Valley" passport event where members cross-refer for a stamp-card prize — last year 600+ residents went through the trail in 30 days.

Will my member listing show up on Google?

Yes. Member profiles use schema markup, structured data, and earn link equity from chamber pages — typical members rank for at least 3 new local keywords within 90 days. We also auto-publish your member status to your Google Business Profile if you connect it, which shows the "Chamber Member" trust signal in search results.
