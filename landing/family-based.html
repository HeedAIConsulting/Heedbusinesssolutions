Common questions

Family Services FAQ

Do you handle background checks for businesses that work directly with children?

Yes. Daycares, pediatric specialists, kids' coaches, and family-facing programs are required to maintain LiveScan-verified staff for every individual listed. We display the verified-fingerprinted badge and re-verify annually as part of renewal. If you don't have LiveScan in place, we can connect you to a low-cost partner to set it up.

Can a family-law attorney be cross-listed in the Professional Services Guide?

Yes. Family-law firms get cross-listing in both the Family Services Guide and the Professional Services Guide at no extra cost — and the Concierge surfaces you for both "family attorney" and "divorce lawyer in Woodland Hills" queries. It's a meaningful boost in surface area without doubling membership fees.

Is there a parents-only audience I can reach?

Yes — our Parent Resource Guide newsletter goes to ~6,400 self-identified West Valley parents monthly. Silver members get one parent-targeted spotlight per year; Gold gets quarterly. Open rates run 38–44%, well above industry average for newsletter audiences.

How are you working with hospitals and pediatric practices?

We have referral partnerships with Tarzana Medical Center, Kaiser Woodland Hills, and the West Valley pediatric collective. New-patient packets include the Parent Resource Guide; our Wellness Resource Network displays your member listing on their family-resource pages. It's the closest thing to a warm handoff a chamber can offer.

Can I sponsor or host a family event?

Yes — every Bronze+ member can co-host a Family Activity event (story-time, kids' fitness, weekend craft) at the Chamber boardroom or at one of our park partners. Gold members get a presenting slot at the annual Family Day at Warner Park (8,000+ attendees), which is consistently our highest-conversion event of the year.

Does the Concierge handle bilingual parent queries?

Yes — the Concierge runs in five languages, and a third of our parent queries come in Spanish, Russian, Armenian, or Farsi. We tag your listing with the languages your staff serves so you surface for those searches. It's one of the most underused advantages we have, and it converts disproportionately well.
