Common questions

Activity & Recreation FAQ

Can a fitness studio actually win a Community Choice Award?

Yes — and they consistently do. The 2025 winner in the Wellness category was a Tarzana yoga studio that joined at Bronze 14 months earlier. Members get nominee placement, voting promotion across our full audience, and a winner's badge that runs in the Weekly for 12 months. A win is genuinely the best ROI a small studio can get from a chamber, year for year.

How does the Chamber support new-member promotions or grand openings?

Every member at Bronze and above gets a ribbon-cutting (we bring the giant scissors and District 3 council photographer), a press release distributed to local media, and a featured slot in the next West Valley Weekly. Studios using this typically book 30–50 trial classes from the press alone — usually their highest-ROI single moment of the year.

What's the deal with event presenting opportunities?

We run 200+ events per year — from breakfast meetings to the Food & Wine Event to Family Day at Warner Park. Activity-based members get first refusal on the active-lifestyle slots: presenting the 5K, leading the kids' fitness corner, hosting the post-F&W stretch session. These are typically reserved 6–8 weeks in advance and waitlist heavily.

Do you support drop-in vs. membership-based pricing models?

Yes — your member profile shows pricing tiers (drop-in, class pack, monthly, annual) and links straight to your booking system (MindBody, Vagaro, ClassPass, Glofox, Mariana Tek all supported). The Concierge filters by price model, so a resident asking "yoga drop-in near Tarzana" only gets matched with studios that offer it.

Outdoor and adventure businesses — is there a fit?

Absolutely. We have hiking guides, mountain bike clubs, and kayak rental services as members — they cross-list in the Family Activities Guide and the CityLoop Local Resource Guide. Outdoor businesses tend to do exceptionally well at our Earth Day and summer kickoff events, which draw outdoor-curious residents we don't always reach in-studio.

Can I run an exclusive member-only deal?

Yes. The Member-to-Member Deals board is one of our most-used member tools — fitness members regularly run "first month free for new members of any other Chamber business." Cross-traffic from those deals is consistently 20–35% conversion, and it builds the kind of internal flywheel where 450 businesses send each other clients.
