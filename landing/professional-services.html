Common questions

Professional services FAQ

Do attorney advertising rules allow Chamber membership listings?

Yes. California Bar Rule 7.2 permits firms to be listed in chamber-of-commerce directories and rated business associations. Our directory includes the standard "This is an attorney advertisement" disclosure when required, and we never imply specialization unless your firm is State Bar-certified.

Will the AI Concierge actually refer my firm?

Yes — every Silver, Gold, and Platinum member is included in the Concierge's referral pool with priority weighting. When a resident asks the Concierge for a family-law attorney in Tarzana or a CPA in Warner Center, your firm appears in the answer with a tap-to-call and your member intake form. We log every referral and you can see them in your member dashboard.

How is this different from BNI or LegalShield referral networks?

BNI is closed and one-firm-per-category. The Chamber is open, broader, and our referrals come from residents searching the directory or asking the Concierge — not from members trading leads. Most members we surveyed run both, and find the Chamber's lead source is their second- or third-largest channel after referrals.

Can I host CLE or continuing-ed events at the Chamber?

Absolutely. Members at Silver and above get one complimentary boardroom evening per year for client seminars, CLE, or continuing-ed. Wine, projector, and a member-pricing list of caterers included. Most firms use it as a soft-touch client appreciation event during tax season or year-end.

Do you cap how many of each profession can join?

No. Tarzana alone has dozens of CPA practices and family-law firms — we are not in the business of picking winners. The Concierge ranks by member tier, response rate, and reviews, not by exclusivity. The directory has filters so residents find the right fit.

What if my firm is bilingual (Spanish, Russian, Armenian, Farsi)?

We will tag your listing with the languages you serve, and your firm will surface for residents searching the Concierge in those languages — a major advantage in our Valley's communities. Roughly a third of Concierge sessions are in a language other than English.
